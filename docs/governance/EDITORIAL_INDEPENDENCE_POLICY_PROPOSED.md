# Stratellar editorial independence policy

**Proposed, not adopted.**

Drafted 2026-11-28 for the founder's review. This file is a repository document only. Nothing on the public site links to it, quotes it, or relies on it, and no public copy may cite it as a policy in force until the founder approves a final text and settles the decisions listed at the end.

## Draft policy

**Purpose.** Stratellar LLC publishes records and research intended for citation and reuse. This policy states how the firm keeps that work independent of its commercial interests.

**Independent research.** Stratellar chooses the subjects of its public records and published studies, and its conclusions are its own. No client, licensee, or funder, should Stratellar have one, and no government or company named in a study, approves, edits, or previews public research before publication. Stratellar may ask a named organization to confirm a fact. Analysis and conclusions stay with Stratellar.

**Commercial work and editorial judgment.** A data license grants use of the records as published. What Stratellar records, how a record is classified, and when it changes are editorial decisions that stay outside any license. Advisory work is contracted separately. Public records and public studies are commissioned, scheduled, and revised by Stratellar alone, whatever an advisory client asks. Coverage, placement, and favorable treatment are never for sale.

**Evidence integrity.** Every factual claim traces to a source the reader can open. Stratellar prefers official and primary documents, attributes each statement to its publisher, and separates announcements, commitments, disbursements, and operating results. Government and company claims are reported as claims. Unknown fields stay marked as unknown. Analysis and scenarios are labeled as such and are kept apart from the source-linked record.

**Corrections.** Anyone may report an error by email, with the record or page and the source that shows it. Stratellar will review each report against the primary source. A confirmed error will be corrected in the record, and the change will be dated and described. Material corrections to a published study will be noted on the study. A correction will be made on the same terms whether the report comes from a client, a company named in the record, or a member of the public.

**Conflicts of interest.** Stratellar declines advisory work that would require it to change or withhold public research. Where an advisory engagement or a financial interest held by the firm or its founder bears directly on the subject of a public study, Stratellar discloses on that study that a relationship exists, or withholds the study from publication. The firm accepts no payment, gift, or hospitality from the subject of a study in connection with that study.

**Client confidentiality.** Stratellar names an advisory client or licensee only with written consent. Information received in confidence is used only for the engagement it was provided for. It stays out of the public record and public research, and it stays with the client that provided it. Public records are built only from sources that any reader can obtain.

**Ownership and review.** The founder is responsible for this policy and will review it once a year. Changes will be dated.

## Decisions the founder must make before adoption

1. **Disclosure trigger.** Are licensee and advisory relationships disclosed on related research, and at what level: existence only, category of client, or named with consent? The draft says "that a relationship exists."
2. **Funding statement.** Can Stratellar state publicly that it has no outside owner, sponsor, or funder, and will it commit to disclosing one if that changes? The About page currently says only that no university, institution, or outside organization sponsors the company or directs its research.
3. **Personal holdings.** Does the founder hold, or agree to forgo or disclose, positions in companies or commodities that the research covers? The draft requires disclosure or non-publication.
4. **Fact-check previews.** May a named company see specific facts before publication, as drafted, or is there no pre-publication contact at all?
5. **Advisory-to-public boundary.** May non-confidential findings from an engagement later inform public research, and after what delay or consent?
6. **Corrections log.** Is there a public, dated corrections page, or are corrections noted only on the affected record and study? The site makes no public promise about corrections until this is settled.
7. **Gifts and travel.** A flat refusal, as drafted, or a disclosed-and-capped rule for site visits and conferences?
8. **Annual review and ownership.** Confirm the founder as sole owner of the policy and the review interval.
9. **Publication.** Where the approved policy lives (`/about#independence` or its own page) and whether About links to it.
10. **Contact address.** The single contact address in `lib/site.ts` is a university-hosted mailbox, printed beside every email pathway. It bears on the independence statement and is a one-line change once a company mailbox exists.
11. **Formation facts.** The site states only that the company was founded in 2026. A place or month of formation, and any `foundingDate` or `foundingLocation` in structured data, wait for the founder to confirm the exact legal wording.
